What is new in 1.10.0

Morning: a card on the home screen opens six short timed practices (silence, affirmations, picturing the day, moving, reading, writing). To see it: unlock the app, tap the card, tap "Begin". "Next" moves through each practice without waiting for the timer. The last practice is a page to type on or write on by hand; "Finish" saves it to the journal.

Evening: from 7pm the same card offers a four-part wind down. At any other hour, open Morning and tap "Looking for the evening wind down?" at the bottom of that screen.

Reminders: each routine has a "Remind me" switch on its setup screen. Turning it on asks for notification permission and schedules one local notification a day at the chosen time. Notifications are scheduled on the device with the system's local notification API; the app has no server and sends no push notifications. They are off by default.

The Morning screen credits the book that inspired the routine (Hal Elrod's The Miracle Morning) and states that the app is independent and not affiliated. The name of that routine is not used as a feature name or in the store listing.

"What you need lately" (Settings, and the Affirm practice) is three tap-only questions that suggest affirmations. Nothing is typed, and the answers stay on the device.

Unchanged: there is no account and no sign-in. The app makes no network connections; everything stays on the device. The passcode is created by the user on first launch.
