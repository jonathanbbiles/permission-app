New: Sounds.

Rain, ocean waves, brown noise and white noise, for when you want something to rest in. Pick one, choose how long (anywhere from 10 minutes to 10 hours) and press Play. It keeps going with the screen off, fades out when the time is up, and stops by itself if you start a recording, so your voice entries stay clean.

Every sound is made right on your phone. Nothing is downloaded, and nothing leaves it.

You will find Sounds on the home screen.

Everything still stays on your device.
